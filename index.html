Building templates

Dhaka 6-storey residential, 3 × 2 bays
v1.0.1BDCC-BY-4.0
The New Building wizard's starting values: an RC frame of 3 bays of 6 m by 2 bays of 5 m, 6 storeys (ground 3.6 m, typical 3.2 m), C27.6 concrete and B420DWR bars, isolated footings, residential use (occupancy category II) in Dhaka on an assumed site class SD until a soil report gives one. The generator sizes the members; designs follow BNBC 2020.
